# Community conduct

ApplyFlow adopts the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in project spaces and when representing the project. Welcome contributors across identities, abilities and experience levels. Critique the work respectfully and give others room to learn. Harassment, threats, unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of private information are grounds for intervention.

Report an incident privately using the maintainers’ designated contact when listed in the repository. Until that channel is available, use GitHub’s private abuse-reporting controls for conduct on GitHub. Moderators should protect the reporter’s privacy, address conflicts of interest and explain decisions to the people affected. Depending on impact or recurrence, responses can include a correction, a warning, a temporary exclusion or a permanent ban.
